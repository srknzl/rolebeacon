VALIDATED SOURCE

What about Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Apple, Netflix, or LinkedIn?

Google Careers and Amazon Jobs have dedicated first-party connectors. Microsoft, Meta, Apple, and Netflix still require their own adapters, so RoleBeacon reports that limitation instead of pretending the URL is supported. LinkedIn Job Alerts through your own Gmail account remain an optional coverage source; authenticated LinkedIn pages are never scraped.
